# J&amp;K 3030 keychain

An original retro-futurist companion piece for the Archive Module. It evokes the geometric, stretched-type atmosphere discussed for the project without reproducing protected album artwork.

## Finished size

- 72 × 30 × 4 mm
- 5.4 mm keyring hole

## Parts

- Black PLA base
- One AMS face job: iron-gray PLA face, black PLA lettering, and a red PLA heart
- Separate yellow PETG HF signal-line insert

The yellow insert is mechanically fitted into the gray face rather than printed against PLA.

## Editable wording

Change `NAME_TEXT` and `STATUS_TEXT` at the top of `models/john_3030_keychain.scad`.

## Assembly

1. Press the completed gray/black/red face into the black base recess.
2. Press the yellow signal insert into its matching channel.
3. If either fit is loose, use a tiny amount of thick CA on the underside only.
4. Add a split ring through the 5.4 mm reinforced hole.
