# J&amp;K 3030 keychain

An original retro-futurist companion piece for the Archive Module. It evokes the geometric, stretched-type atmosphere discussed for the project without reproducing protected album artwork.

## Finished size

- 72 × 30 × 4 mm
- 5.4 mm keyring hole

## Print jobs

- `01_keychain_black_gray_red_ams.3mf` — one fused AMS PLA body: black base, iron-gray face, black lettering, and red heart
- `02_signal_yellow_petg.3mf` — separate Yellow PETG HF signal-line insert

The base and face are printed together and permanently fuse during the AMS job. The yellow insert is mechanically fitted into the gray face rather than printed against PLA.

## Editable wording

Change `NAME_TEXT` and `STATUS_TEXT` at the top of `models/john_3030_keychain.scad`.

## Assembly

1. Print the fused black/gray/black/red AMS body.
2. Print the yellow PETG insert separately.
3. Press the yellow signal insert into its matching channel.
4. For keychain durability, add one tiny drop of thick CA beneath the yellow insert.
5. Add a split ring through the 5.4 mm reinforced hole.
