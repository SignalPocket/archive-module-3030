# PETG Flexure Test V2

The original 3.0 mm-thick security bar was too stiff. These neutral-looking cable-clip coupons test longer, thinner PETG flexures before the final latch is revised.

Print all three together in Yellow PETG HF:

- `flexure_120.stl` — 1.20 mm thick
- `flexure_150.stl` — 1.50 mm thick
- `flexure_180.stl` — 1.80 mm thick

Settings: 0.16 mm layers, 5 walls, 100% infill, no supports. Print each coupon with its broad flat face on the build plate. Use the same orientation for all three.

After cooling completely, hold the thick 10 mm anchor block. Press the small raised pad at the opposite end by only 2 mm, then release. Do not bend the coupon sharply and stop immediately if it whitens, cracks, or makes a snapping sound.

Choose the thickest coupon that moves about 2 mm with comfortable finger pressure and returns fully straight. Do not use the original stiff latch.
